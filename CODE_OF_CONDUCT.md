# Code of Conduct

Saath is about kindness, so this space should be kind too.

- **Be respectful.** No insults, mocking, or harassment of anyone.
- **No judgement** about anyone's feelings, culture, religion, gender or background.
- **Keep it safe.** Don't share personal problems or anyone else's private information here.
- **No medical claims or advertising.**

Comments or contributions that break these rules may be removed, and repeat offenders blocked. To report a problem, open an issue or contact [@maddym041-tech](https://github.com/maddym041-tech).
